Researcher
├── create and manage their own profile
├── upload and manage publications
├── create and join research projects
├── register for conferences
├── track citations
└── view collaboration network

Institution Admin
├── manage their institution's profile
├── view all researchers in their institution
├── view institution's publications and projects
├── generate institution reports
└── manage departments

Reviewer
├── review submitted publications
├── add review comments
├── approve or reject publications
└── view assigned publications only

System Admin
├── manage all users and roles
├── view all institutions
├── generate platform-wide reports
├── manage conferences
└── full access to everything
